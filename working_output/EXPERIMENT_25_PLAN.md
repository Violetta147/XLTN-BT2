# Experiment 25: spectral-flatness rejection gate

- Rollback/accepted baseline before this experiment: `93dca9c` (AMDF + energy gate + median5; EXP24 gate rejected).
- Source of hypothesis: read-only subagent review. A high spectral-flatness ratio may identify broadband/noise-like frames that pass the current energy and AMDF gates; code search found no existing flatness feature or gate.
- Hypothesis: rejecting only high-flatness frames otherwise predicted voiced can reduce spurious F0 estimates while preserving most voiced recall.
- Single variable: high spectral-flatness cutoff `T_SF`. Compute power bins `P = abs(rfft(frame * hann))**2` and flatness `exp(mean(log(P + 1e-12))) / (mean(P) + 1e-12)` using all `rfft` bins. Apply only to baseline predicted-voiced frames. Keep AMDF, pitch/energy threshold fit, F0 lag, median5, frame length 25 ms and hop 10 ms unchanged. Include an explicit no-gate control.
- Training-only selection: outer four-fold LOFO on the four training files. For each outer fold, use inner three-fold LOFO; fit existing pitch and energy thresholds on the two inner-training files; derive numeric `T_SF` candidates from quantiles `q ∈ {0.50, 0.60, 0.70, 0.80, 0.90, 0.95}` of spectral flatness on their baseline predicted-voiced frames; compare these gates and no gate on each inner-held-out file. Select q by highest mean per-file macro F1; ties by higher balanced accuracy, higher V recall, then fewer rejected frames. Refit baseline thresholds on all three outer-training files and recalibrate the chosen q-quantile cutoff from their predicted-voiced frames; apply once to the outer holdout. If no gate wins, apply none. Held-out labels are evaluation-only.
- Keep only if all outer results versus the no-gate median5 baseline pass: mean macro F1 and balanced accuracy each decline by at most 0.005; mean recall V declines by at most 0.01; mean F0mean MAE rises by at most 0.10 Hz; mean F0std MAE improves by at least 0.25 Hz; aggregate NumF0 drops by no more than 2%; total FP_SIL is at most 3; and no held-out file's F0mean/F0std MAE worsens by more than 1.0 Hz. Otherwise document failure and retain baseline.
- Report all required class metrics, F0mean/F0std MAE, NumF0 and FP_SIL per file and in aggregate. Assert no-gate parity against EXP24. Only training data; no TEST or Google Drive. Four-file LOFO is limited internal evidence, not independent validation.
- Preregistered result only: keep plan and implementation/run in separate commits, preserve outputs, and record exact command/environment/input paths.
